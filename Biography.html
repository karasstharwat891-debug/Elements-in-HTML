Dr.Magdy Yacoub

Dr. Magdi Yacoub is a famous Egyptian heart surgeon and medical scientist. He was born on November 16, 1935

, in Belbeis, EgyptHe graduated from Cairo University Medical School in 1957

later moved to Britain to continue his medical training.


Dr. Yacoub became one of the pioneers of heart and lung transplantation. He worked for many years at Harefield hospital, in London

where he helped develop a major heart-transplant program and performed many complex heart operations.

He also worked on treatments for children with congenital heart problems.


In addition to his medical career, Dr. Yacoub has dedicated much of his work to helping people.

He founded the Chain of Hope charity and the Magdi Yacoub Heart Foundation

which established the Aswan Heart Centre in Egypt to provide free

heart treatment and conduct medical research.


Dr. Magdi Yacoub is widely respected for his contributions to medicine ,research, and humanitarian work

His life has inspired many people around the world to use science and medicine to help others.

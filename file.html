The Great Pyramids of Giza

The pyramids of Giza are one of the most famous ancient landmarks in the world

They were built in Egypt over 4500 years ago as tombs for pharaohs

The three main pyramids are the Great Pyramid of Khufu, the Pyramid of Khafre, and the Pyramid of Menkaure .Located near Cairo,

they attract millions of visitors and remain a symbol of ancient Egyptian civilization, engineering, and history.

Pyramids were often also named in ways that referred to solar luminescence.

For example, the formal name of the Bent Pyramid at Dahshur was The Southern Shining Pyramid, and that of Senusret II at El Lahun was Senusret Shines
